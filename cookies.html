Cookie Policy

How cookies and similar technologies are handled on the Home Screen Captain website.

Last updated: September 14, 2026
Current website setup: This Home Screen Captain website is intended to operate without advertising cookies, behavioral tracking cookies, or analytics pixels.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that websites can store on a browser or device. Similar technologies can include local storage, pixels, and software development kits used for analytics or advertising.

2. Cookies used by this website

The Home Screen Captain website does not intentionally set cookies for advertising, behavioral profiling, cross-site tracking, or analytics.

If the website is later updated to use analytics, embedded media, advertising, or other technology that places non-essential cookies, this Cookie Policy and any required consent controls should be updated before those technologies are activated.

3. Hosting and essential technical processing

The company that hosts this website may automatically process basic technical information needed to deliver and secure the site, such as IP address, request time, browser information, and security logs. Those hosting practices are controlled by the hosting provider and may be subject to its privacy policy.

4. Third-party links

This website may link to Google Play, support resources, or other third-party websites. When you open a third-party site, that site may use its own cookies or similar technologies. Home Screen Captain does not control third-party cookies.

5. The Android app

This Cookie Policy applies to the website. The Home Screen Captain Android app does not require a Home Screen Captain account and is designed not to use website cookies to build a Home Screen Captain user profile. The app may display advertisements. Third-party advertising or other services used through the app may apply their own technologies and privacy practices.

6. Changes to this Cookie Policy

If our website technology changes, we may update this Cookie Policy. The latest version will always show the current "Last updated" date.

7. Contact

Questions about cookies or website privacy can be submitted through the Support page.
