HOME SCREEN CAPTAIN — GOOGLE PLAY PUBLICATION CHECKLIST
Last updated: September 14, 2026

These pages are policy-oriented templates, not a guarantee of legal or Google Play approval.
They must match the app's real behavior and every SDK/library included in the release.

1. PRIVACY POLICY
- Host privacy.html at a public, active HTTPS URL.
- Add that URL in Google Play Console.
- Make the Privacy Policy reachable inside the app as well.
- Keep the policy consistent with the Play Console Data safety form.

2. DATA SAFETY
- Review the final APK/AAB and every third-party SDK.
- "No data collected" is appropriate only if the app and included SDKs actually satisfy Google's definition.
- Search, weather, coupon, crash-reporting, analytics, advertising, or other SDKs can change the answer.
- Update the Data safety form whenever app or SDK behavior changes.

3. ACCESSIBILITY SERVICE
- A launcher is not automatically an "accessibility tool."
- If AccessibilityService is used for coupon/shopping assistance, complete the Play Console Accessibility declaration.
- Show a separate, prominent in-app disclosure immediately before requesting Accessibility permission.
- The disclosure must explain what information the service can access and exactly why.
- Require affirmative user consent.
- Website text or the Privacy Policy alone is not enough.
- The feature should remain optional and permission should be reversible.

Suggested in-app disclosure:
"Shopping Assistance uses Android Accessibility Service only when you enable it.
It can detect supported shopping pages opened in supported browsers so Home Screen Captain can identify and display relevant coupons or offers.
This access is not used to read passwords or payment details, build a browsing profile, or monitor unrelated activity.
The feature is optional and can be turned off at any time in Android Accessibility settings."
[Not Now] [Agree & Continue]

4. ADVERTISING
- If the app displays ads, make sure the store listing and Privacy Policy do not make conflicting claims.
- Review the final advertising SDK configuration before completing the Play Console Data safety form.
- Keep ads appropriate for the app's content rating and follow Google Play advertising policies.

5. OPTIONAL KEYBOARD
- Keep keyboard enablement user-initiated.
- Do not collect or transmit typed content if the privacy policy says you do not.
- If any spelling, voice, AI, analytics, crash, or cloud feature transmits text, update the policy and Data safety form first.

6. STORE LISTING
- Use real screenshots that accurately represent the app.
- Avoid absolute claims such as "100% safe," "guaranteed safe," or "collects zero data" unless they are literally true for the complete app and all SDKs.
- Factual wording such as "No account required" and "Home Screen Captain does not sell personal information" is safer when accurate.

7. SUPPORT
- Make sure the developer support email in Google Play Console is active.
- If you want a visible email on support.html, replace the generic instruction with the verified support email.

8. BEFORE RELEASE
- Test install, launcher switching, uninstall, keyboard disable, Accessibility disable, and search settings reversal.
- Verify Privacy Policy, Terms, Cookie Policy, Support and FAQ links all work over HTTPS.
