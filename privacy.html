Privacy Policy

A clear explanation of how Home Screen Captain handles information.

Last updated: September 14, 2026
Privacy summary: Home Screen Captain does not require an account and is designed so that the app itself does not collect, sell, or store personal information about users. Some optional features connect to third-party services, which may process information under their own privacy policies.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Home Screen Captain handles information when you use the Android application and this website.

2. No account or profile

Home Screen Captain does not require registration, sign-up, a user profile, or a Home Screen Captain account. We do not ask for your name, date of birth, phone number, payment information, or financial information to use the app's core launcher features.

3. Information Home Screen Captain does not collect

The app is designed so that Home Screen Captain does not collect or store personal data on our servers. In particular, we do not use the app to collect:

  • account credentials or passwords;
  • payment card, bank, credit, loan, or investment information;
  • the contents of your contacts, emails, SMS messages, photos, or files;
  • text typed with the optional Home Screen Captain keyboard;
  • precise location history;
  • browsing history for advertising profiles; or
  • personal information for sale to data brokers or advertisers.

4. Installed apps and launcher functionality

To function as a launcher, Home Screen Captain may need to identify apps installed on the device so it can display app icons, shortcuts, and an "All Apps" view. This information is used to provide launcher functionality on the device and is not collected or stored by Home Screen Captain as a user profile.

5. Advertisements

Home Screen Captain may display advertisements. Home Screen Captain itself does not require an account and does not sell personal information.

Ads may be delivered by third-party advertising services. Depending on how an advertising service is configured, that provider may process limited technical information needed to deliver, measure, secure, or prevent fraud in connection with ads. Any such third-party processing is governed by the provider's own privacy practices.

6. Optional Accessibility Service

Home Screen Captain may use Android's Accessibility Service only after you choose to enable the optional shopping-assistance feature. The service can access page context from supported shopping pages in a supported browser so the app can determine whether relevant coupon codes or offers can be displayed.

Home Screen Captain does not use this permission to collect a general browsing history, read passwords, read payment-card details, build advertising profiles, or monitor unrelated activity. Page context accessed for this feature is used only for the disclosed shopping-assistance purpose and is not retained by Home Screen Captain as a personal browsing history.

The Accessibility Service is optional. You can refuse permission or disable it later in Android settings without creating a Home Screen Captain account.

7. Optional keyboard

If you enable the optional Home Screen Captain keyboard, text input is used to provide typing functionality on your device. Home Screen Captain is designed not to collect, transmit, or store the content you type through the keyboard.

8. Weather, browsers, and other third-party services

Some app features may display information or open services provided by third parties. When you choose to use a third-party app, website, browser, weather provider, advertising service, or other service, that third party's privacy policy applies to its processing. Home Screen Captain does not control the privacy practices of third parties.

9. Support communications

The app does not automatically collect personal information. If you voluntarily contact us for support, we will receive the information you choose to provide in your message, such as your email address and the content of your request. We use that information only to respond to the support request, maintain necessary support records, protect the service, or comply with legal obligations.

10. Data selling and advertising profiles

Home Screen Captain does not sell personal information. We do not use the app to build advertising profiles based on contacts, messages, keyboard input, browsing history, or financial information.

11. Security

Because Home Screen Captain is designed not to maintain user accounts or a server-side personal-data profile for core launcher use, the amount of personal data held by us is minimized. We still use reasonable administrative and technical measures for any information voluntarily provided to us, such as support messages.

12. Children

Home Screen Captain does not require users to provide personal information through an account. We do not knowingly use the app to collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information was provided to us through a support request by a child, contact us so we can review the request.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when features, third-party services, legal requirements, or data practices change. The updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.

14. Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact method shown on the Support page or the developer contact information displayed on the Home Screen Captain Google Play listing.
